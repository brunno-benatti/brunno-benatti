01 · About

Understand before rewriting.

My work is most valuable when the system already exists, the context is complex, and an incorrect change costs more than a few extra lines of investigation.

I work hands-on across investigation, architecture, implementation, integration, testing and delivery.

My verified professional surface includes internal ERP work, payment gateways, automated PIX integration, REST APIs, webhooks, Laravel e-commerce and administrative systems, NetSuite integration and existing-system evolution.

I prefer bounded scopes, explicit assumptions and incremental changes over unnecessary rewrites.
